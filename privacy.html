Privacy policy

VPS Backups Rclone is a private utility operated for its owner's personal backup needs. Updated 26 October 2026.

Data and purpose

The utility accesses Google Drive using drive.file solely to create, read, verify, update and delete the backup repository files available to this application. Backup content is selected by the owner. The utility does not request full-Drive access, Gmail access, contacts, or advertising data.

Restic encrypts backup content on the owner's server before it is uploaded. Google Drive stores the resulting encrypted files. The owner keeps the recovery password separately. OAuth credentials are stored on the owner's systems with restricted access; they are not published on this website.

Use and sharing

Google user data is used only to provide and maintain the private backup function. It is not sold, used for advertising, or used to train artificial-intelligence models. Google Drive is the storage provider; Google processes data under its own policies. The backup utility does not send plaintext backup content to this website or to an AI model.

Retention and control

The intended policy keeps seven daily recovery points, removing older unneeded backup data only after successful backup verification. The owner can stop the schedule, revoke the application's access in their Google Account, and delete its files from Drive. Revoking access does not itself delete existing backup files. Files may remain until the owner removes them if the utility cannot connect.

This website and contact

This informational website has no forms, analytics, or Google sign-in. Its hosting provider may process ordinary website access logs under its own privacy policy.

Questions and requests are handled by the application owner through the support email shown on the Google authorization screen.

The use of information received from Google APIs follows the Google API Services User Data Policy, including its Limited Use requirements.
