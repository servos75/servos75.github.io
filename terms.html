VPS Backups Rclone is intended only for its owner's private backup and recovery operations. Updated 26 October 2026.

This website does not offer a public backup service, user accounts, subscriptions, or payments. The owner chooses which data to back up and authorizes access to their own Google Drive.

Availability depends on the owner's server, network, Google account, available storage and valid authorization. A configured schedule alone does not guarantee that a backup succeeds. The owner should review backup results and test recovery.

Recovery requires access to the stored backup files and the separate restic recovery password. The password should be kept outside the backed-up server. This utility does not provide a password-recovery service.

The owner can stop using the utility and revoke its Google access at any time. Existing backup files remain under the owner's control in Drive until removed.

The support contact is the application owner, identified by the support email on the Google authorization screen.
